How does Generate choose?
  1. It leaves as few people as possible with nothing to attend in a slot.
  2. Then it seats people in sessions they rated highly: ★3 = 1 point, ★4 = 2, ★5 = 3. Hosts get no points for their own session.

A session runs only if the minimum number of people other than its hosts are seated. Pinned sessions skip this rule.
